EverWeave Consulting Project

Your consulting assignment

Evaluate the Summit Trail Outfitters opportunity and recommend how EverWeave should proceed.

On this page

Your roleRequired analysisFinal deliverablesProfessional standard

Your Role

Your consulting team has been engaged by EverWeave to evaluate the Summit Trail Outfitters opportunity and recommend how the company should proceed.

Your work should demonstrate thoughtful business judgment, use evidence from the case and exhibits, and recognize that strong recommendations must consider both opportunity and tradeoffs.

There is no predetermined correct recommendation. Your responsibility is to develop and defend the course of action your team believes is best supported by the available information.

Required Analysis

1. Major Facts

Identify the most important facts that influence EverWeave’s decision.

Focus on information that matters to the recommendation rather than simply summarizing the case.

2. Problem Statement

Clearly state the primary business problem EverWeave must address.

Your problem statement should reflect the larger business issue rather than simply restating the Summit proposal.

3. Alternatives

Develop 3–5 realistic alternatives EverWeave could consider.

For each alternative:

  • Explain what the option would involve.
  • Identify important advantages.
  • Identify important disadvantages or tradeoffs.
  • Identify meaningful risks related to EverWeave’s resources, values, relationships, and current business position, and consider whether the alternative is realistic.

4. Choice and Rationale

Select the alternative your team recommends.

Explain the criteria your team used to evaluate the alternatives and why your recommendation is stronger than the other alternatives considered.

Support your reasoning with evidence from the case and exhibits. Your recommendation should demonstrate that your team has considered financial, operational, people, customer, and relationship implications where relevant.

5. Implementation Plan

Explain how EverWeave should put your recommendation into action.

Your plan should identify:

  • Major actions EverWeave should take.
  • A logical sequence or timeline.
  • People or groups who would be responsible for or affected by the implementation.
  • Resources the company would need.
  • Any conditions, safeguards, or priorities that should guide implementation.

Your recommendation is not complete until you explain how it could realistically work.

6. Implementation Risks

Identify the most important risks created by your team’s recommendation.

For each significant risk:

  • Explain why it matters.
  • Describe how it could affect EverWeave.
  • Recommend how the company could reduce, manage, or respond to the risk.

Avoid treating risks as an afterthought. Every reasonable business decision involves uncertainty and tradeoffs.

Final Deliverables

Your consulting team will submit:

Executive Summary

A concise, one- to two-page professional summary of the decision, alternatives considered, recommendation, rationale, implementation approach, and major risks.

Consulting Presentation

A 6–8 minute professional presentation communicating your team’s recommendation to EverWeave’s leadership.

Peer Evaluation

Each team member will complete the required peer evaluation reflecting individual contribution to the consulting project.

Professional Standard

Approach this work as though you were presenting it to an actual client.

Your recommendation should be clear enough to understand, realistic enough to implement, and well supported enough that your team would be willing to defend it in front of EverWeave’s founders.

The goal is not to identify the answer you believe your instructor wants.

The goal is to demonstrate your best thinking.
